Cannabis legal age: Germany and Canada.

Both countries set the national minimum at 18. Germany adds a restricted tier until 21; Canada lets each province set its own age, and nine of ten set it higher.

Method

How this was prepared.

Each statute was taken from its government's official consolidation and loaded into the Clausehound statute corpus with section-level anchors, so every figure above traces to the section that sets it. No secondary sources were used. The territories are not yet covered. Germany's KCanG consolidation notes an amendment of 22 October 2026 that its publisher has not finished processing. This brief is a summary for discussion, not legal advice.

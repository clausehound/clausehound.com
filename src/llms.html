--- permalink: /llms.txt eleventyExcludeFromCollections: true --- # Clausehound > {{ site.description }} Clausehound Inc. is a Toronto company building legal software since {{ site.foundingYear }}. Its clause taxonomy is patented ({{ site.patent }}, "Clause Taxonomy System and Method for Structured Document Construction and Analysis"). ## What it does - Breaks statutes, regulations and contracts into a tree of clauses with stable identities, so they can be tagged, compared and cited. - Keeps every historical version of the law. For example, it holds 84 versions of Ontario's Employment Standards Act since 2002, and about 4,000 statutes and regulations in total. - Compares any two versions clause by clause. - Checks that every section a model cites exists in the version in force on the date asked about, and flags it if not. - Runs contract review against playbooks of prompts, with a lawyer approving each finding. Exports to Word with tracked changes. ## How to connect - App for lawyers and legal teams: {{ site.appUrl }} - GraphQL API for applications. - MCP server for AI agents (Claude or any MCP client). Tools include find_statutes, list_statute_versions, search_statute_text, compare_statute_versions and fill_schedule. - Runs hosted by Clausehound, in a customer's cloud, or on the customer's own servers. SOC 2 audited. ## Coverage Deepest corpus is Canadian: federal, Ontario, Québec, British Columbia, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, New Brunswick and municipal bylaws. Subject areas include employment, occupational health and safety, corporate and securities, privacy, tax, environmental, real estate, insurance and pensions. ## People - Joshua Koudys, Chief Technology Officer and co-inventor: {{ site.joshUrl }} - Rajah Lehal, founder and co-inventor. ## Related - Cobalt ({{ site.cobaltUrl }}) is a separate law firm that runs its legal services on Clausehound.

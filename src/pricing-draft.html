Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

What is a playbook?

The checks a lawyer would run on one kind of contract: what a good clause looks like, why it matters and example language to fix it. Clausehound runs them against your document and shows you where each one lands.

Who stands behind the findings?

Lawyers build and verify the corpus: Canadian statutes and regulations, tracked version by version. Every finding cites the clause it is about and the source it rests on, so you can check it yourself.

What does "changed since it was signed" mean?

Tell us when a contract was signed and we list the changes to the law since then that bear on it, each with its citation and effective date.

What counts as a document?

One contract reviewed against one playbook. Re-running the same contract after you edit it doesn't count again.

Is this legal advice?

No. It's a review tool. When you need a lawyer to stand behind the result, add Lawyer-verified.

Do I need my own AI account?

No. The app works out of the box. Only Enterprise deployments can bring their own model.

Can I change plans?

Upgrade at any time. Downgrades take effect at renewal.

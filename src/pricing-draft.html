Questions

The fine print, in plain words.

Do I need my own AI model?

Builders bring their own model and tokens, so Clausehound never bills you for inference. App plans work out of the box.

What counts as attributable revenue?

Gross revenue from products that call the Clausehound API or MCP server. Products that don't use the engine don't count.

Can I split my products to stay under the threshold?

No. Every company under common control is one account, and the threshold counts your whole portfolio.

What does fair use mean on hosted plans?

Hosted accounts are rate-limited so one product can't slow down everyone else. If yours outgrows that, we'll talk first and help you move to a dedicated or self-hosted deployment. There are no usage fees either way.

How do I report revenue?

Quarterly, once you're over the threshold. Below it, there's nothing to file. Standard audit rights apply.

Do referrals pay?

Yes. Send a customer to a Clausehound subscription and earn 20% of its first year.

Who owns my data?

You do. Export it whole at any time, and get 6–12 months' notice before we end a service.

Can I sell my product?

Yes. The agreement moves with a sale of your company or of a single product line, and survives a change of control at Clausehound.

The rules

The same terms for every builder.

  • One account, affiliates included. Every company under common control is one account. The threshold counts your whole portfolio, however many products or companies it spans.
  • Only attributable revenue. Gross revenue from products that call the Clausehound API or MCP server. Products that don't use the engine don't count.
  • Fair use on our servers. Hosted accounts are rate-limited so one product can't slow down everyone else. If yours outgrows that, we'll talk first and help you move to a dedicated or self-hosted deployment. There are no usage fees either way.
  • Simple reporting. Report quarterly once you're over the threshold; below it, there's nothing to file. Standard audit rights apply.
  • Referrals pay. Send a customer to a Clausehound subscription and earn 20% of its first year.
  • Your data is yours. You own your documents and data, export them whole at any time, and get 6–12 months' notice before we end a service.
  • Your product can be sold. The agreement moves with a sale of your company or of a single product line, and survives a change of control at Clausehound.
